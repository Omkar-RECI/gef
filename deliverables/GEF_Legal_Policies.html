Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Global Entrepreneurs Forum respects your privacy and handles information responsibly. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at info@gefofficial.com.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to gefofficial.com and Global Entrepreneurs Forum (GEF) digital communications. GEF is a flagship initiative of RECI India Foundation. It explains how we handle personal information when you browse our website, contact us, or use an event registration link made available through this website.

2. Information we may collect

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, organisation and message when you contact GEF. Our website may also collect limited technical information such as device, browser, pages visited and approximate location through cookies or analytics tools, where enabled.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, manage event interest and registrations, improve website performance, communicate relevant GEF updates where permitted, and protect the website from misuse. We do not sell personal information.

4. Event registration and third parties

Event registration may be completed through an embedded third-party ticketing or registration service. Information submitted within that service is handled under that provider's terms and privacy practices, in addition to this policy. Please review the provider's notices before entering payment or registration details.

5. Sharing and retention

We share information only where reasonably necessary to operate GEF, work with authorised service providers, meet legal obligations, or protect our rights and users. We retain information only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose, legal requirements, or legitimate operational records.

6. Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent for personal information held by GEF, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. You can also manage most browser cookies through your browser settings.

7. Security and updates

We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy as our website, events, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This policy applies to paid registrations for Global Entrepreneurs Forum events, a flagship initiative of RECI India Foundation. Event-specific terms shown during registration form part of this policy and will prevail if they differ.

1. Registration fees and payment

Registration fees, inclusions, applicable taxes and payment methods are displayed during checkout. A registration is confirmed only after successful payment and confirmation from the applicable registration platform. Payments are processed by the designated third-party payment or ticketing provider; GEF does not store card or banking credentials.

2. Cancellation by a participant

Cancellation or refund eligibility, if any, is governed by the event-specific terms shown at registration. Where no separate cancellation window is stated, confirmed registration fees are non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or where GEF cancels the event. Requests must be sent from the registered email address before the stated deadline.

A delegate substitution may be considered before the event, subject to the event-specific deadline, delegate category, verification requirements and written approval from GEF. Tickets may not be transferred, sold or used for commercial resale without prior written approval.

GEF may reasonably modify the programme, speakers, venue, format or schedule. If an event is postponed, registrations may be moved to the revised date. 5. Refund processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible. Processing times depend on the payment provider and financial institution. Any non-refundable third-party charges will be disclosed where applicable before a refund is processed.

GEF is not responsible for travel, accommodation, visa, insurance or other personal costs incurred by a delegate, including where an event programme or schedule changes, except where required by applicable law.

Please contact GEF first to resolve a payment concern before initiating a chargeback. For payment, cancellation, transfer or refund requests, email info@gefofficial.com with your registration name, event name and confirmation reference.

This policy is subject to applicable law. GEF may update it before an event to reflect operational, regulatory or event-specific requirements. The version published on this page applies from its stated update date, without limiting rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
